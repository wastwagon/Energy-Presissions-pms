## Executive summary

Energy Precisions has invested in a **professional digital platform** — corporate website, e-commerce shop, lead capture, project management system (PMS), and content administration — that rivals or exceeds most solar installers in Ghana.

**The platform is ready. Sales are not yet following at the rate we need** because:

1. **Visibility** — The market does not yet know we exist at scale (Google, social media, reviews, content volume).
2. **Trust signals** — Online proof (Google reviews, video testimonials, published pricing) is underdeveloped.
3. **Sales follow-through** — Digital leads require a disciplined response and closing process (operations, not only technology).
4. **Payment path** — Large-ticket installs (GHS 140,900 – 447,900) need clearer staged payment and financing messaging.

This document summarises **what has been built**, **what remains to be done**, and a **realistic timeline** for when we should expect measurable results.

> **Key message for leadership:** We have completed the *infrastructure* phase. The next phase is *market activation* — a combination of marketing spend, content, reviews, sales discipline, and targeted website enhancements. Results are achievable, but they follow a 6–12 week ramp; they are not instant.

## 1. Strategic context — Ghana solar market

### Market opportunity

| Factor | Relevance to Energy Precisions |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| Unreliable ECG grid & outages | Strong demand for hybrid backup + lithium storage |
| Rising electricity tariffs | ROI story for residential and commercial buyers |
| High generator running costs | Hybrid solar is a credible alternative |
| Growing middle class & SMEs | Target market for 6.5–20 kVA packages |
| Mobile money & Paystack adoption | Enables online deposits and shop sales |

### Competitive reality

Most Ghana solar competitors win on **visibility and simplicity**, not engineering depth:

- They show prices upfront
- They respond fast on WhatsApp
- They collect Google reviews aggressively
- They run Facebook/Instagram ads continuously

Our engineering and package structure are **stronger** than average. Our **market presence** is not yet at the same level.

### Our positioning (recommended)

> **Ghana's hybrid solar specialist — engineered LiFePO₄ packages from GHS 140,900, turnkey installation, Accra HQ with nationwide service.**

## 4. Enhanced growth strategy

The strategy is organised into **five pillars**. Each pillar has specific actions, owners, and expected contribution to leads and installs.

### Pillar A — Search & local visibility (highest-intent channel)

**Objective:** Appear when Ghanaians search "solar installation Accra", "hybrid solar Ghana price", etc.

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Google Business Profile optimisation | Complete profile, weekly posts, 20+ install photos |
| Google review campaign | Request review from every completed install; target 30+ reviews in 90 days |
| Local SEO content | Accra, Kumasi, Tamale pages + add Tema, Cape Coast in Q4 |
| Blog publishing | 2 Ghana-specific articles per month minimum |
| Search Console monitoring | Track queries, fix low-click pages |
| Display package "From" prices | Filter serious buyers; improve ad landing page conversion |

### Pillar B — Social media & paid advertising (awareness + demand)

**Objective:** Reach homeowners and SMEs in Greater Accra and Ashanti Region who are not yet searching.

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Content calendar | 4–5 posts/week (Facebook + Instagram); minimum 1 Reel/week |
| Content themes | Install proof, package education, ECG pain, customer stories, team credibility |
| Paid social test | GHS 3,000–5,000/month; geo-target Accra then Kumasi |
| Landing pages | Drive ads to `/solar-packages` or `/solar-accra`, not homepage |
| WhatsApp as primary CTA | Every ad and post ends with chat or free site assessment |
| WhatsApp Business catalog | 6 package tiers with photos and From prices |

### Pillar C — Trust & social proof (conversion enabler)

**Objective:** Overcome Ghana market scepticism about solar installers.

| Action | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Google reviews on `/reviews` page | Connect live rating via CMS |
| Video testimonials | 3–5 customer videos (phone quality acceptable) |
| Portfolio case studies | Real installs with location, tier, savings estimate |
| Showroom / office visibility | Signage, demo system, QR to WhatsApp at Haatso |
| Warranty & staged payment clarity | On packages page, quote PDF, and financing page |

### Pillar D — Sales process & lead management (closing the funnel)

**Objective:** Convert digital interest into site visits, quotes, deposits, and installs.

| Stage | Target | Process |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Lead → first contact | < 1 hour (business hours) | WhatsApp call or voice note |
| Contact → site visit booked | 40–60% | Offer 2 specific dates |
| Site visit → quote sent | 90%+ within 48 hours | PMS quotation PDF |
| Quote → deposit | 25–40% | Staged 30/40/30; Paystack deposit link |
| Deposit → install | 95%+ on schedule | Project management |

**Lead status tracking in PMS:** New → Contacted → Survey booked → Quoted → Deposit received → Installing → Complete → Review requested

### Pillar E — Partnerships & offline marketing (accelerator)

**Objective:** Build referral channels beyond digital.

| Channel | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Electricians & installers | Commission per closed referral |
| Estate agents & developers | Bulk pricing for new estates |
| Hotels & guest houses | B2B outreach; commercial case studies |
| Vehicle branding | Install vans with phone + "Free site survey" |
| Print / QR flyers | High-traffic Accra areas → load calculator |

## 7. When to expect results (realistic forecast)

Marketing results follow a **ramp curve**, not a switch. Below are honest expectations for a Ghana solar SME starting from limited digital visibility.

### Results timeline

| Timeframe | What to expect | Typical metrics |
|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Weeks 1–2** | Setup only; minimal new leads | Infrastructure live; GA4 tracking; 5–10 reviews added |
| **Weeks 3–4** | First paid/social leads appear | 5–15 enquiries/month; learning which ads work |
| **Weeks 5–8** | Lead volume stabilises | 15–30 enquiries/month; first installs from digital channel |
| **Months 3–4** | Organic + paid compound | 25–40 enquiries/month; 2–4 installs/month from marketing |
| **Months 5–6** | SEO blog content begins ranking | Organic traffic +10–30% month-on-month |
| **Months 6–12** | Predictable pipeline if sales process holds | 30–50+ enquiries/month; 3–6 installs/month |

### Assumptions behind these numbers

- Paid ad budget: **GHS 3,000–8,000/month** (scaled after Month 2)
- Sales response within **1 hour** during business hours
- **At least 1 site visit booked per 3–4 qualified leads**
- **At least 1 install per 8–12 qualified leads** (10–15% lead-to-install)
- Average deal value: **GHS 180,000–250,000**

### Revenue illustration (conservative)

| Scenario | Installs/month | Approx. monthly revenue |
|----------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Break-even marketing | 2 | GHS 360,000 – 500,000 |
| Target (Month 4+) | 3–4 | GHS 540,000 – 1,000,000 |
| Growth (Month 8+) | 5–6 | GHS 900,000 – 1,500,000 |

> **One GHS 200,000 install covers 2–3 months of lean digital marketing spend.**

### What will NOT happen

- Viral overnight growth from website alone
- High Google rankings in Week 1 (SEO takes 3–6 months)
- Installs without sales follow-up — **technology generates leads; people close deals**
